El Salvador Tax Incentives: Who Qualifies for Up to 10 Years of Relief?

DESCRIPTION
Qualifying new investments in San Salvador's defined Historic Center may receive up to ten years of income-tax relief. Not every business qualifies, and a landlord's approval doesn't automatically lower a tenant's taxes.
