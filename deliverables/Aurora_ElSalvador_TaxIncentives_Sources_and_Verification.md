## 4. Details found but deliberately left out of the video

- **Municipal taxes:** APLAN may recommend a partial municipal-tax exemption of up to five years, subject to approval by the San Salvador municipal council (search excerpt, S4). Excluded because it was unverified and outside the income-tax focus.
- **Regulation dates:** published in the Diario Oficial on 4 Nov 2025 and in force from 12 Nov 2025 (S2, S3). Excluded because the dates are unverified.
- **Enabling law:** in force since 2023 (S4). Excluded because the date is unverified.
